# Room 09 — Nandhavanam Farm (raw)

## Story
Bought 2019, foothills of Kodaikanal (start of Western Ghats). Was still in IT — onsite, Guidewire. Land was an answer before the question. Left IT for good Sept 2024 → moved out of city, built home mid-woods. Natural farming, slow learning. Today: centre of gravity for family, food, work, silence. Also a brand, school, promise.

## Philosophy (user voice)
- Live close to nature
- Grow our own food
- Self-sustainable + healthy lifestyle
- Hopeful living for next gen — no chemicals/pesticides, real nourishment
- Train young generation: natural farming + sustainable living + technology + modern practices

## What we grow
Coconuts (+ value-added products), Bananas, Poultry, Native-breed cows and bulls.
